# Community conduct

Discuss code and evidence respectfully. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
abuse and publication of another person's private information are not welcome.
Explain disagreements with reproducible examples and leave space for correction.

Do not post credentials, private conversations or participant data in issues or
reviews. Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
For other concerns, contact a repository maintainer through the contact options
on their GitHub profile; do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation. This policy
does not imply an independent review team or a guaranteed response time.
